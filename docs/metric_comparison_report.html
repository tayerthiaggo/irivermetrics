Fixed references

APSEC and LPSEC use A_ref and L_ref; LPI and MESH use fixed A_total. Raw area, length and perimeter remain audit context, not invented response equations.

Valid observations

Occurrence, recurrence, hydroperiod and TCF divide by valid observations/months. Refuge area also enforces min_valid_obs, so missing imagery is not silently counted as dry.

Dependency gates

Channel, dynamics and connectivity metrics are skipped when channel geometry, HY anchors, dual composites, fixed nodes or graph inputs are unavailable.

Interpretation guards. Width is a planform morphology signal, not depth. Extent contraction is a monthly surface-water slope, not a hydrograph recession constant. Resolution sensitivity, fixed versus moving denominators, valid support, and RC/TCF positioning against DCI and PC/IIC must travel with results.
