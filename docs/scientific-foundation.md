# Scientific Foundation

HydroFragments builds upon published literature in ecohydrology, remote sensing, and river landscape ecology.

## Core Publications

1. **Tayer et al. (2023) — Journal of Hydrology**
   - *Title:* Ecohydrological metrics derived from multispectral images to characterize surface water in an intermittent river.
   - *DOI:* [10.1016/j.jhydrol.2023.129087](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jhydrol.2023.129087)
   - *Summary:* Developed multispectral surface water metrics (extent, persistence, fragmentation, morphology) to characterize surface water refugia in dryland intermittent rivers.

2. **Beesley et al. (2023) — Freshwater Biology**
   - *Summary:* Evaluated ecological responses of fish populations to pool persistence and disconnection in intermittent river corridors.

3. **Tayer et al. (2021) — Remote Sensing of Environment**
   - *Summary:* Validated Water Observations from Space (WOfS) accuracy and cloud/shadow masking algorithms across northern Australian river systems.

4. **McFarlane et al. (2020) — CSIRO Technical Report**
   - *Summary:* Framework for surface-water persistence and refugia assessment in the Fitzroy River catchment.

5. **Bourke et al. (2022) — Hydrological Processes**
   - *Summary:* Groundwater-surface water interactions and dry-season pool persistence mechanisms in alluvial river channels.
